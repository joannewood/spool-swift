Settings & Keyboard Shortcuts

Settings (⌘,)

Open Spool's Settings window with ⌘,, or Spool › Settings… from the app menu.

SettingWhat it does
Watch for changes automaticallyPeriodically re-scans your folders for changes made outside Spool (a file renamed or deleted in Finder, for instance). Turn off to freeze your library exactly as it was last scanned.
Rescan intervalHow often that scan runs, from 1 to 60 minutes. Files added or changed while Spool is running are usually picked up immediately regardless, via live filesystem watching — this interval is the defensive fallback.
Automatically extract archivesSkips the Archives review queue and extracts every newly-found relevant .zip as soon as it's discovered. Doesn't apply to .7z/.rar — Spool can't extract those itself; see Reviewing Duplicates, Archives & Suggestions.
